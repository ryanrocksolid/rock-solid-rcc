Why Rock Solid RCC

From site review to final roll, the process stays clear.

  1. ConsultationWe review site use, traffic and structural needs.
  2. PlanningWe coordinate layout, drainage, thickness and mix requirements.
  3. MixMaterials are mixed on-site using a portable pugmill to maintain a precise mix design.
  4. PlaceThe concrete is placed with precision equipment suited to the project.
  5. FinishCuring compound is applied, and concrete can be saw cut for a finished look.

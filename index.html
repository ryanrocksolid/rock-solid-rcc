WHY RCC

Concrete performance. Asphalt-style placement.

RCC combines a dense concrete pavement with efficient placement and compaction. It is suited to high-load sites where durability, speed and lifecycle value matter.

HOW WE WORK

From site review to final roll, the process stays clear.

  1. ConsultationWe review site use, traffic and structural needs.
  2. PlanningWe coordinate layout, drainage, thickness and mix requirements.
  3. ProductionFresh RCC is produced on-site with high-capacity equipment.
  4. PlacementPrecision paving and vibratory compaction create the finished surface.
  5. InspectionDensity, finish and project details are checked before closeout.

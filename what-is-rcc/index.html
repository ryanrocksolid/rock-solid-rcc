What is Roller Compacted Concrete?

A dense, high-strength pavement system installed with efficient paving and vibratory compaction.

Talk About Your Project →
THE BASICS

Concrete strength with an efficient paving process.

RCC is a low-slump concrete mixture of aggregate, Portland cement and water. It is placed with precision equipment and compacted with vibratory rollers to create a dense pavement surface.

01

Mix

Materials are blended to the project’s required mix design.

02

Place

The material is placed with paving equipment suited to the job.

03

Compact

Heavy vibratory rollers achieve the required density.

04

Finish

Curing and joint-cutting complete the pavement system.

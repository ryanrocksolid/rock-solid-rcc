NORTON • RCC SOLUTIONS

Plan for grain, equipment and seasonal movement

Northwest Kansas sites can combine grain receiving, equipment storage and livestock support. A site plan that shows truck routes, turning areas, loading points and drainage gives the design team a clearer basis for deciding where a hard pavement surface may be useful.

  • FeedlotWhere a livestock operation needs paved feed lanes or service access, evaluate vehicle loading, runoff and cleanout as part of one coordinated design.
  • Grain StorageReceiving aprons and bin access can be planned around unload positions, conveyors, truck queues and room for future storage.
  • Commercial & IndustrialDealership and repair yards may need a mix of parking, loading and equipment paths; map turning movements before setting pavement limits.
  • MunicipalFor public parking or street work, coordinate pavement details with utility covers, tie-ins, drainage, traffic control and bid documents.
LOCAL PROJECT PLANNING

Coordinate storage access with equipment circulation

In the Norton area, note harvest delivery peaks, equipment turning paths, loading areas, existing pavement edges and water flow before setting project limits. These details help the owner and designer compare pavement options against actual site operations.

RCC is one option for suitable projects; pavement thickness, base preparation, drainage and joints need project-specific design. Share a site plan and operating needs with our team to discuss fit.

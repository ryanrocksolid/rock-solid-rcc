GRAIN STORAGE

Keep the site ready when harvest demands it.

Grain facilities see heavy trucks, loaders, augers and seasonal traffic. RCC creates a hard, durable surface that reduces rutting and keeps equipment moving in changing weather.

  • Ground-pile and flat-storage pads
  • Loadout and scale areas
  • Equipment and drive lanes
  • Drainage-conscious site layouts

THE CONSTRUCTION PROCESS

How RCC paving is installed

Understand the typical project stages and the decisions that shape a successful placement.

Roller compacted concrete (RCC) is a low-slump concrete pavement placed with paving equipment and compacted with vibratory rollers. The construction sequence is coordinated so that material production, delivery, placement and compaction keep pace with one another. The exact plan depends on project size, weather, access, mix requirements and site conditions.

1. Prepare the site and base

Before paving, the team reviews grades, drainage, subgrade and base conditions, utilities, tie-ins and access. The foundation is prepared to meet the project specifications. Accurate grading matters: pavement elevations need to connect to existing surfaces and direct water as intended.

2. Coordinate mix production and delivery

RCC mixture proportions are established for the design requirements and materials. On-site production and hauling are planned around placement rates and site logistics. The project team manages consistency, supply and traffic so the paving operation can proceed in a controlled sequence.

3. Place and compact the RCC

Paving equipment spreads the low-slump concrete to the required width and depth. Rollers then compact it to achieve the specified density and surface. The paving crew coordinates placement and compaction timing, edges, tie-ins and construction joints according to the approved project plan.

4. Protect and cure the finished pavement

After compaction, curing and protection follow the project specifications. Saw-cut joints may be used as required by the design. The owner and contractor should agree on access limits, inspection, testing and the criteria for returning the surface to service.

Construction steps, testing, curing and opening timelines are controlled by the project specifications and conditions. This overview is educational and does not replace the approved plans.

What to ask before work begins

  • What preparation, testing and acceptance criteria are in the contract documents?
  • How will deliveries, equipment access and adjacent operations be coordinated?
  • What weather conditions may affect placement or curing?
  • Who approves inspection results and reopening to traffic?

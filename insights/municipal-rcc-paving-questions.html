PUBLIC WORKS

Municipal RCC paving project questions

Align pavement requirements, public access and construction coordination before work begins.

Municipal roller compacted concrete projects can involve public streets, shoulders, parking areas, utility yards and other paved spaces. Early coordination helps a municipality and its project team define scope, access, specifications and responsibilities. RCC should be evaluated against the project's engineering requirements and procurement process.

Clarify the project requirements

Identify the pavement limits, intended use, design traffic, tie-in points, drainage features, utilities and applicable standards. Confirm which party is responsible for geotechnical information, pavement design, testing, inspection and acceptance. If the project has funding, permitting or bid-document requirements, include those milestones in the schedule.

Plan for residents and essential services

Map traffic detours, pedestrian access, emergency routes, business access, deliveries and waste collection. Determine how notices will be shared and who will communicate schedule changes. For work near utilities or public facilities, coordinate with the relevant departments and service providers before mobilization.

Coordinate construction and quality control

Discuss staging limits, weather contingencies, material testing, inspection hold points, curing protection and criteria for reopening the pavement. Include the sequence for saw cutting, joints, transitions and connections to existing roads or structures in the contract documents.

Make the information easy to review

A concise plan set, traffic-control approach, project contact list and decision schedule help keep the owner, engineer and contractor aligned. Clarify how field questions will be documented and who can approve adjustments to scope or schedule.

Municipal pavement design and procurement requirements vary. Follow the engineer's plans, contract documents, applicable codes and agency procedures.
